### Thank you — what the study was about

Every reply came from the same language model. What changed from turn to turn was (a) what the model was told about your emotion — **the emotion you had just reported**, a **deliberately opposite emotion**, or **nothing at all** — and (b) whether the agent **stated** how you seemed to feel or **asked** you about it instead.

We are measuring how much a wrong emotional guess costs compared with how much a right one helps, and whether asking rather than asserting softens the cost. Some replies therefore misjudged you on purpose. Your webcam frames were analysed only to estimate a facial-expression label for comparison with what you reported; they were never stored. Automatic emotion recognition from faces is imperfect and scientifically contested, so nothing it estimated about you should be taken as fact.
